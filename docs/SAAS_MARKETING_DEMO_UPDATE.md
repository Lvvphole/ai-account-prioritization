# SaaS Marketing Demo Update

Approved visual direction for the public AI Account Prioritization landing page.

## Intent

Present the application as a credible AI business SaaS product rather than an engineering demo. The public page should communicate the customer problem, the product workflow, the live demo, revenue context, evidence, human control, and enterprise safeguards in plain business language.

## Product-truth constraints

- Do not present the priority score as win probability.
- Do not claim the ranking model predicts which account is most likely to close.
- Do not invent customer logos, adoption claims, or performance outcomes.
- Keep customer-facing actions human-approved.
- Keep verified CRM evidence visible in the product preview.
- Preserve the existing deterministic ranking and guardrail implementation behind the customer-facing language.

## Approved visual direction

- Dark navy SaaS surface with blue-to-violet accents.
- Large two-column hero with a real product-shaped dashboard preview.
- Live-demo CTA above the fold.
- Business metrics displayed as supporting context, not fabricated proof.
- Marketing language centered on sales priorities, evidence, next actions, and revenue work.
- Technical implementation jargon removed from the primary marketing hierarchy.
- Enterprise controls retained as a customer benefit rather than internal architecture language.
